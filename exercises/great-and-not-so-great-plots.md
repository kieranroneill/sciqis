# Great and not-so-great plots

_Practice_

1. Open at least 10 random papers among the [recent arXiv:quant-ph submissions](https://arxiv.org/list/quant-ph/recent?skip=0&show=2000).
2. Find a plot that you particularly like.
3. Find a plot that you think leaves significant room for improvement.
4. Take a screenshot of each of them.
5. Submit the great plot on the [#yay-plots](https://discord.com/channels/1527267313563205773/1534671804176793651) channel and the not-so-great plot on the [#nay-plots](https://discord.com/channels/1527267313563205773/1534671833339658290) channel. Along with the figure, you should write a brief but well-considered comment about why you like it or don't.
6. Feel free to emoji-react to your classmates' submission if you agree or comment in a thread (right-click → Create Thread) if you disagree.
